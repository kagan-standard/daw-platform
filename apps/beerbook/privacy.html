Local storage on your device

We use browser local storage and session storage (and similar mechanisms) to keep you signed in, remember UI preferences, and support first-party analytics session identifiers. These mechanisms are controlled by your browser or device settings.

Sources of information

  • You, when you register, sign in, create content, upload photos, or communicate with us.
  • Our identity provider, when you authenticate (for example profile claims such as email consistent with your OIDC scopes).
  • Automatic collection, when you use the Service (technical data such as IP address, user agent, and first-party analytics events sent from the app to our API).

How we use information

  • Provide, operate, and improve BeerBook, including ratings, catalog features, venues, crews, and achievements.
  • Maintain accounts, authenticate users, secure the Service, detect abuse, and enforce our terms.
  • Display public-by-design social features: profiles, ratings, and related activity are intended to be visible to other users and, for some endpoints (such as follower lists), may be accessible without authentication as implemented today.
  • Operate first-party analytics to understand usage, referrals, and product performance.
  • Comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.

Legal bases (UK / EEA / Switzerland)

  • Legitimate interests — securing the Service, improving features, and first-party analytics, balanced against your rights (you may object where applicable).
  • Consent — where required for optional processing (for example, where you grant browser location permission for venue verification).
